Smart Paste Typing mimics natural keystrokes to enter clipboard content into any text field. Use it on websites that restrict standard pasting, or in workflows that need manual-entry simulation.

Key Features
Simulated Typing: Converts clipboard text into real keyboard events, for forms and login screens with "no-paste" restrictions.

Instant Action: Click the extension icon to start typing into the focused input field at once.

Quick Command Shortcut: Start paste-as-typing with Cmd + Shift + V (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + V (Windows). You can change the shortcut.

Context Menu: Right-click an editable field and select Paste as Typing.

Smart Field Detection: Automatically finds the active editable area or input box.

Your Speed: Pick Slow, Normal, or Fast, or set your own minimum and maximum delay.

Progress and Stop: A badge on the toolbar icon shows the character count. Trigger the extension again while it types to stop.

New in 3.0
A new logo, and a new extension page in light and dark mode with a quick guide, your current shortcut, a practice field, and all settings. Your current settings stay the same.

How it Works
The extension only activates when you trigger it. It injects a temporary script into the current page to find the active cursor and "type" your clipboard text character by character.

Privacy & Security
No External Servers: Your clipboard data is processed locally on your machine.

Zero Data Retention: We do not upload, store, or share your clipboard or page content.

On-Demand Execution: The extension only interacts with a page when you click the icon, use the shortcut, or select the menu item.
