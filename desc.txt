Smart Paste Typing mimics natural keystrokes to enter clipboard content into any text field. It’s the perfect solution for websites that restrict standard pasting or for workflows that require manual-entry simulation.

Key Features
Simulated Typing: Converts clipboard text into real keyboard events, bypassing "no-paste" restrictions on forms and login screens.

Instant Action: Click the extension icon to immediately begin typing into the focused input field.

Quick Command Shortcut: Fire off a paste-as-typing command instantly with Cmd + Shift + V (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + V (Windows).

Smart Field Detection: Automatically identifies the active editable area or input box without requiring manual selection.

How it Works
The extension only activates when you trigger it. It injects a temporary script into the current page to find the active cursor and "type" your clipboard text character-by-character.

Privacy & Security
No External Servers: Your clipboard data is processed locally on your machine.

Zero Data Retention: We do not upload, store, or share your clipboard or page content.

On-Demand Execution: The extension only interacts with your browser when you explicitly click the icon or use the shortcut.
