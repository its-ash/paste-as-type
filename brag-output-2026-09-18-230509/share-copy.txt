SHARE COPY — Smart Paste Typing
================================

X / Twitter
-----------
Some forms don't take paste. Smart Paste Typing doesn't ask — it types.

Trigger it with ⌘⇧V / Ctrl+Shift+V from any editable field. Simulated keystrokes, processed locally, nothing uploaded.

LinkedIn
--------
Some forms block pasting entirely — copied text just won't land in the field.

Smart Paste Typing solves this by simulating real keystrokes: your clipboard text types itself in, character by character, exactly like a human would. Trigger it from the toolbar icon, the right-click menu, or a keyboard shortcut (⌘⇧V / Ctrl+Shift+V) — it only activates when you ask it to, and everything runs locally in your browser.

Paste anything. Type it naturally.
